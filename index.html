Our Go-to-Market Approach

ONE NEIGHBORHOOD.
THEN THE NEXT.

We're starting where relationships matter most: local. The goal is to build a concentrated network, prove the economics, and create a repeatable model before expanding.

PHASE 01 / CONNECT

Build the foundation.

Partner with a focused group of local businesses. Secure sponsors and launch approved Counter and Reach activations.

PHASE 02 / LEARN

Measure what matters.

Evaluate engagement, sponsor value, partner experience, and campaign economics. Test relevant offers and customer journeys.

PHASE 03 / EXPAND

Grow the network.

Connect complementary touchpoints, explore new activations, and replicate successful approaches in adjacent neighborhoods.
